Coral reefs are diverse marine ecosystems that provide ecological functions and economic and social benefits across over 100 tropical countries. Warming driven by climate change is a primary threat to coral reefs and is increasing the frequency of global coral bleaching events. Here we reassessed the IUCN Red List status of 892 reef-building coral species, first assessed in 2008 1. Because species-level population trends are unavailable for most reef-building corals, population reductions were inferred using proxies based on global coral cover trends and projected annual severe bleaching under SSP2-4.5, both over three decades, adjusted using trait-based estimates of species vulnerability. Reaction-diffusion competition drives anomalous relaxation of vitrimers

Original Paper | Polymers | 2026-10-14 20:00 EDT

Makayla R. Branham-Ferrari, Shinian Cheng, Alexei P. Sokolov, David S. Simmons

Since their discovery in 2011, vitrimers–covalent associative network polymers–have challenged the traditional understanding of soft matter relaxation dynamics. Unlike typical glass-forming liquids, the viscous relaxation of vitrimers can be entirely decoupled from their underlying structural dynamics. In addition, the origin of the Arrhenius viscosity in vitrimers in the presence of super-Arrhenius structural relaxation behaviour has been of high interest due to its potential to provide readily reprocessable high-performance plastics. Here we identify two types of transient networks in the relaxation dynamics of vitrimers based on the ratio of atomic displacement scales required for bond exchange to those required to relax a segment. In systems where bond exchange requires only subsegmental motion, our results indicate that network relaxation is governed by a competition between chemical exchange reactions and segmental diffusion. This competition produces the signature network-segment decoupling of the vitrimers and also drives a commonly observed crossover between Arrhenius and super-Arrhenius behaviour. This suggests an explanation for long-standing puzzling features of vitrimer dynamics and establishes a foundation for rational vitrimer design.

Silicon spin qubits offer a promising path to scalable quantum computing due to their compatibility with industrial semiconductor manufacturing. A key requirement for scaling quantum processors is the ability to perform high-fidelity operations in parallel across many qubits. However, simultaneous control remains a major challenge in silicon devices, as fidelities typically degrade under parallel operation. Here we identify microwave-drive-induced a.c.-Stark shifts as the dominant source of this degradation in a five-qubit silicon spin array. We address this by introducing a scalable mitigation protocol based solely on pairwise phase calibrations. During parallel five-qubit operation, fidelities remain above the widely targeted fault-tolerant threshold of 99.9%. By maintaining high-fidelity control during simultaneous operation, shared qubit-control lines can support the development of large-scale semiconductor quantum processors.

Gate-tunable chiral spin mode in WSe2/WS2 moiré superlattices

Research Article | 2d materials | 2026-10-15 03:00 EDT

Zhexu Shan, Wenjian Su, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Shiyao Zhu, Yuanfeng Xu, Yanhao Tang

The interplay between many-body and spin-orbit coupling (SOC) effects can lead to previously unobserved phenomena in solids. Chiral spin modes (CSMs) are collective spin excitations that arise from such interplay and connect states with opposite chirality. Here, we report a gate-tunable CSM that occurs between conduction SOC-split minibands in near-0°-twisted WSe2/WS2 bilayers. This mode manifests as a sharp resonance with giant Raman efficiency in the pseudovector-symmetry channel of the Raman spectra. By varying the filling, the CSM transitions from a chiral spin exciton to an excitonic polaron. The spin-flip nature is directly confirmed by the Zeeman effect, and the filling dependence indicates a charge-transfer insulator at unity filling. Our results demonstrate that transition metal-dichalcogenide moiré superlattices are a fertile platform for exploring exotic low-lying collective excitations.

When calving cliffs at the margins of Earth’s ice sheets exceed a critical height, runaway retreat may be initiated in a process called the marine ice cliff instability. However, sufficiently tall ice cliffs have rarely been observed. To address this gap, we measured the characteristics of 15,330 ice cliffs across Earth’s ice sheet margins using laser altimetry. Finally, we quantify nuclear-spin storage by transferring coherence from the electron spin to the 31P nuclear spin, obtaining a phase-memory time of 20.8 ms, approximately 1,300 times that of the electron spin. These results establish an electron-nuclear molecular spin register in which quantum sensing and storage are integrated within a single molecule.

Metastable nanoparticle cores amplify single-ion quantum level crossings

Physical Sciences - Article | Magnetic properties and materials | 2026-10-14 20:00 EDT

Xiang Zhao, Shuangchi Liu, Tongwei Zhang

Classical size dispersion and experimental artefacts have long obscured the discrete quantum energy levels of individual magnetic sites in disordered nanoparticle ensembles. We report a field-periodic, phase-coherent switching resonance–robust under instrumental and processing controls–in the first-order reversal curve (FORC) response of biogenic magnetoferritin at 2 and 5 K. The period, μ₀ΔH = 11.9 ± 1.6 mT, is invariant and phase-coherent (p ≈ 10⁻³), and the resonance disappears above the blocking temperature. Cross-correlation shows the ridges tilt in proportion to the core remanence slope (ρ ≈ 0.78): the structure tracks the internal hysteretic state of the core, not the applied field, excluding instrumental and processing origins. The spacing gives an axial zero-field splitting D = 0.016 ± 0.002 K, identifying the active unit as an individual surface Fe³⁺ ion (S = 5/2), whose relaxation is exponentially amplified by cores thermally poised at their switching threshold (Γt ≈ 1). The resulting size-temperature window (TB/9.0 ≤ T ≤ TB) accounts for the thirty-year absence of resolved resonances in conventional magnetometry and predicts the geometric signature recovered in independent data at 6.9σ. This establishes a paradigm where macroscopic metastability serves as a quantum amplifier, enabling benchtop spectroscopy of single-ion quantum states in inherently disordered nanomaterials.

Article | Electrocatalysis | 2026-10-14 20:00 EDT

Simantini Nayak, Gyanprakash Mishra, Saswati Mishra, Brijesh Patel, Satyabrata Sahoo, Ashutosh Rath

MXenes are promising electrocatalysts for sustainable hydrogen generation, but establishing reliable structure-activity relationships to achieve phase purity and control surface terminations remains challenging. Here, we report phase-pure Ti3C2-F and Ti3C2-O MXenes synthesised via a molten-salt process, achieving purities >99.6%. These findings not only elucidate the underlying reaction mechanisms but also demonstrate the potential of termination-controlled MXenes for efficient energy conversion.
